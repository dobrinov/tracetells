Why Trace exists

Built for agents, not dashboards.

Analytics tools were built for people with time to read dashboards and watch replays. Now a coding agent can change your product in minutes, but it can't watch a replay or dig through reports. Trace gives it what it needs: the problems worth fixing, ranked and with evidence, over MCP. Then it checks that each change actually helped.

Made to be read by agents

Summaries and evidence an agent can act on, not charts for a person to interpret.

Made to check every change

When shipping is fast, knowing what worked matters more than ever.

Made without surveillance

Your AI only ever sees patterns, so there is nothing personal to leak into a prompt.

Measure. Fix. Check.

1 · Trace measures

One script tag. It counts what happens on each page and ranks the problems by how many visits they hurt.

2 · Your agent fixes

Claude Code, Cursor or any MCP client reads the problem and its evidence, and makes the change in your code. No agent yet? Each problem is also a written task for a developer.

3 · Trace checks

After the release, Trace compares your goal over 14 days before and after, and says whether the fix worked.

What it shows you

What you'd ask analytics and an error tracker, plus the frustration you'd hunt for in replays, from one script.

Frustration

Dead clicks, rage clicks, forms people give up on, pages they leave fast.

Errors, browser and server

Exceptions and failed requests in the browser, and with the client for Rust, Node.js or Ruby, the server error behind them. Each with the number of visits it broke.

Funnels and goals

Where people drop out of sign-up or checkout, against the targets you set.

Audience

Visitors, bounce and engaged visits without cookies. DAU/MAU and retention for signed-in users, through a hashed id.

Privacy

Patterns, never people.

Trace never records sessions, keystrokes or what's on screen. By default it stores nothing on your visitors' devices, so it doesn't need cookie consent.

What Trace collects, and what it never does

  • No recordings. No replays, no screenshots, no typed text.
  • No cookies by default. Nothing written to the visitor's browser unless you switch on consent mode.
  • Scrubbed twice. Emails, card numbers and tokens are removed in the browser, then again on the server.
  • Hosted in the EU. Finland, or on your own server.

Small, on purpose

A tiny script in the browser, one Rust server, and your goals in a file next to your code. Your agent connects over MCP.
