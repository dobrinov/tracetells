Questions

What happens when I hit the event limit?

Your website keeps working exactly as before. Trace stops recording for the rest of the month and tells you how many events it skipped.

Why is there no checkout?

Paid plans start with a conversation. Write to me and I set your limits by hand.

Can I move from hosted to self-hosted?

Yes. It is the same software. Your trace.json and snippet stay the same; only the server address changes.

Does my agent see my users?

No. Over MCP your agent reads the same aggregate summary you see on the Behaviour page: counts and rates per page, never sessions or people. It analyses with its own model, so the summary goes only where you send it.
