Private by design

I'm not interested in your users. Only in patterns.

No session replay, no screenshots of your pages, no typed text. Trace sees that 18% of visitors tapped a product image and nothing happened, never who they were or what they typed. And it stores nothing on their device, so there's no consent banner to show.

How privacy works
Never collectedInput values, page titles, query strings, page content, recordings.
Scrubbed twiceEmails, cards, IBANs, tokens and phone numbers removed in the browser, then again on the server.
Your own rulesAdmins mask elements, ignore pages and drop fields per website.
Tested with planted secretsA leak page full of fake cards and passwords stores none of them.
Light by design

The collector only takes notes while the page is busy, and does its work when the browser is idle. If anything inside it ever fails, the failure stays inside it.

Questions

Do we need a coding agent?

Yes. Trace measures and shows; the analysis, the fixes and every change happen in your coding agent: Claude Code, Cursor or any MCP client, with the model you choose, local or hosted. Trace never sends your data to a model of its own, and the dashboard tells you exactly what to ask your agent at every step.

Does my agent see my users?

No. Over MCP your agent reads the same aggregated summaries you see: counts and rates per page, never sessions or people. It analyses with its own model, so the summary goes only where you send it.

Will it slow my site down?

The collector is 8.3 KB, only takes notes while the page is busy, and does its work when the browser is idle. A test fails our build if it grows past that.

We already have a consent tool. Does Trace need it?

By default Trace stores nothing on the device, so there's nothing to ask. If your policy is to load nothing before consent anyway, switch it to wait for your consent tool.

What does it cost?

Hosted starts free, and paid plans are set with you. The open-source edition is free with no limits on your own server. See pricing
